Website status

This is an original website design and working frontend for Royal Karate Sports. It is not a live enrollment service. The supplied brand name and domain are used; ownership and hosting have not been verified. Search indexing is disabled until content is approved.

Privacy in this preview

No analytics, advertising cookies or remote form services are included. The Contact form keeps entries only in page memory until you leave or refresh. Downloading a draft creates a text file on your device. No enquiry is transmitted and no class is booked. Do not enter sensitive health information or a child’s personal contact details.

Downloaded files contain the information you typed. Keep or delete them as you choose. A real privacy policy naming the data controller, contact information, purposes, retention periods and applicable rights must be supplied before live collection begins.

Content awaiting confirmation

Akash Kishor Shinde’s biography, portrait, free-training policy and the Dharavi and Pratiksha Nagar locations were supplied by the website owner. Recognition descriptions follow the owner’s supplied account. Newspaper scans, letters and recognition records are now available in the Media archive; their authenticity and official status have not been independently verified. Phone and email were supplied by the owner for the Contact page. Instagram and Facebook links appear in the site footer. Program age ranges, schedules, exact meeting instructions and safeguarding procedures still require confirmation. No additional awards, ranks or medal counts have been invented.

Photography credits

Verify current usage rights and obtain any additional permissions before public use; no model endorsement is implied.

Design independence

Champion’s Karate Club was consulted only for general business-content categories. No competitor photography, contact details, testimonials, affiliations, copy, layouts or visual components are reused.
