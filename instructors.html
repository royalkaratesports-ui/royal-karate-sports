Questions for your instructor

Your first conversation is part of finding the right training environment.

Qualifications and experience

Ask about the instructor’s karate background, current qualifications and experience teaching your age group. Verified Royal profiles will make this information easy to find.

How progress is assessed

Discuss the training approach, feedback, grading requirements and how readiness for more advanced work is determined.

Care, safety and junior students

Ask about supervision, safeguarding, emergency procedures and how activities are adapted to a student’s needs before enrolling.
