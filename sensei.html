About Akash Shinde

Akash Kishor Shinde

Martial Arts With a Mission

Akash Kishor Shinde is a Mumbai-based martial arts practitioner, karate trainer, and social worker dedicated to making martial arts training accessible to children regardless of their financial background.

For Akash Shinde, martial arts is not simply about learning how to fight. It is about developing discipline, confidence, fitness, self-defence skills, respect, and a stronger mindset.

Free Karate Training.No Paid Classes. No Training Fees.

The missionFree trainingThe journeyRecognitionOur vision
His mission is simple
No child should be denied the opportunity to learn martial arts because they cannot afford the fees.

That is why the karate training conducted by Akash Shinde is completely free of charge. There are no paid classes and no training fees.

Access without barriers

Free karate training in Mumbai

Akash Shinde currently provides free karate and martial arts training at two locations in Mumbai.

Dharavi

Ganesh Vidyamandir School, Dharavi

Free karate training for children and students in and around Dharavi.

No training fees
Pratiksha Nagar

Pratiksha Nagar, Mumbai

A second training centre serving children from Pratiksha Nagar and surrounding communities.

No training fees

Students are trained without charging regular coaching or tuition fees. The objective is to give children access to structured martial arts education that might otherwise be financially out of reach.

More Than Karate

Discipline, respect and confidence

The purpose of the training goes beyond kicks, punches, forms, and competition.

Through regular practice, students are encouraged to develop:

  • Self-discipline and confidence
  • Physical fitness and coordination
  • Practical self-defence awareness
  • Focus and mental strength
  • Respect for teachers, parents, and fellow students
  • Sportsmanship and teamwork
  • The confidence to participate in competitive martial arts

Akash Shinde believes that the values learned through martial arts can have an impact far beyond the training ground.

A Journey in Martial Arts

Karate practice and experience

Akash Kishor Shinde has been associated with martial arts for many years as a practitioner, competitor, trainer, and organizer.

The certificates and records documenting his journey include participation and recognition connected with karate and martial-arts competitions dating back several years.

Among the material documenting his martial-arts journey are records relating to the Mumbai Zonal Inter School TEBMA Martial Arts Championship 2016–17, the 23rd WFSKO Open Asian/International Karate Championship in 2018, and subsequent martial-arts and sports recognition.

His work and achievements have also appeared in Marathi and Hindi newspapers.

Giving Children an Opportunity

Working with students

The most important part of Akash Shinde’s work is not an award or certificate.

It is the students.

Through his free classes in Dharavi and Pratiksha Nagar, he aims to create an environment where children can train consistently, learn discipline, improve their fitness, discover their abilities, and build confidence without their family’s financial situation becoming a barrier.

The goal is not simply to create karate competitors.

It is to help develop stronger, more disciplined and more confident young people.

Our Mission

Making karate accessible

To provide quality martial arts and karate training completely free of charge to children and young people, particularly those who may otherwise not have access to professional training.

Our Vision

Community work

To expand free martial-arts education to more communities and give more children an opportunity to learn karate, self-defence, discipline and sportsmanship in a safe and supportive environment.
