Before you begin

Your first class,
made clearer.

Ask the academy↗
Is regular karate training really free?

Yes. Regular karate training conducted by Akash Kishor Shinde is completely free of charge, with no paid classes and no training fees.

Where can I train?

Training is held at Ganesh Vidyamandir School, Dharavi, and Pratiksha Nagar, Mumbai. Confirm the current timetable, exact meeting point and availability before attending.

When can I enroll for a camp?

Summer and winter camps are held twice a year. Enrollment begins one month before each season. Confirm dates, venue and availability with the academy; no current opening is announced here.

Can I start without experience?

Yes, you can enquire as a beginner. A parent or guardian should enquire for a child and confirm age suitability, supervision and what to bring before attending.
