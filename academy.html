About Royal Sports Academy

Martial Arts Training With a Purpose

Free regular karate training. No paid classes. No training fees.

Meet our founder & head coach ↗
Our storyTraining centresOur founder

Royal Sports Academy is a Mumbai-based academy dedicated to making quality karate and martial arts training accessible to children, especially those who may not have the financial means to attend conventional paid classes.

Led by Akash Kishor Shinde, the academy has a simple philosophy:

Martial arts education should not depend on a child's ability to pay.

For this reason, Royal Sports Academy provides its karate training completely free of charge.

There are no paid karate classes and no regular training fees.

Founder & Head Coach

Akash Kishor Shinde

Akash Kishor Shinde is associated with Royal Sports Academy as a martial-arts practitioner, trainer and the driving force behind its free-training initiative.

His martial-arts journey includes participation in karate events and competitions as well as years of involvement in teaching children.

Documents supplied from his martial-arts history include participation and recognition connected with competitions such as the Mumbai Zonal Inter School TEBMA Martial Arts Championship 2016–17 and the 23rd WFSKO Open Asian / International Karate Championship in 2018.

His activities and community work have subsequently appeared in multiple Marathi and Hindi newspaper reports.

In 2023, the Ministry of Home Affairs' published list of Padma Award nominations received also contains two entries under the name Akash Kishor Shinde, Maharashtra. This represents a nomination record rather than receipt of a Padma Award.

Nomination detail supplied by the academy; the original Ministry list has not been independently reviewed for this website preview.

Our Story

The academy's purpose goes beyond teaching martial-arts techniques. It aims to use karate and sports as a means of developing discipline, confidence, physical fitness, self-defence awareness and positive values among young people.

Royal Sports Academy grew from a desire to make structured martial-arts training available to children within local Mumbai communities.

For many families, the cost of professional sports coaching can prevent children from receiving regular training. The academy seeks to remove that barrier.

Instead of building the programme around commercial memberships, its focus is on free training, community participation and student development.

Under Akash Shinde's guidance, children receive regular karate instruction and are encouraged to progress from learning basic techniques to developing the confidence and discipline needed for higher levels of martial arts and competitive participation.

100% Free Karate Training

Training is completely free

No Monthly Fees. No Paid Classes.

One of the defining principles of Royal Sports Academy is that its regular karate training is offered free of cost.

The objective is to give children an opportunity to learn irrespective of their family's financial circumstances.

Students are taught fundamental and advanced aspects of karate and martial arts while developing physical fitness, coordination, discipline and confidence.

Our Training Centres

Royal Sports Academy currently conducts free training in two Mumbai communities.

These centres form the heart of the academy's community-based training initiative.

Dharavi – Ganesh Vidyamandir School

The Dharavi programme provides free karate training to children and students from Dharavi and surrounding areas.

Pratiksha Nagar – Mumbai

The academy also conducts free martial-arts training in Pratiksha Nagar, extending the programme to children from another Mumbai community.

What We Teach

Training at Royal Sports Academy is designed to develop the student as a whole rather than concentrating only on fighting techniques.

The emphasis remains on safe, disciplined and responsible martial-arts education.

  • Karate & Martial Arts Techniques
  • Self-Defence
  • Physical Fitness
  • Strength & Coordination
  • Kata
  • Discipline & Focus
  • Competition Preparation
  • Confidence Building
  • Respect & Sportsmanship
Progress has many forms

From Training to Competition

Students are encouraged to challenge themselves and develop progressively.

Where appropriate, training can help prepare students for martial-arts competitions and sporting events. The academy's broader history includes participation in district, zonal and other martial-arts competitions, as reflected in certificates and newspaper coverage documenting its activities.

Competition, however, is not the academy's only measure of success.

For a beginner, simply gaining confidence can be an achievement. For another student, success might mean improving fitness, learning self-defence, developing discipline or eventually representing the academy in competition.

Our Mission

Our mission is to:

Provide children with quality karate and martial-arts training without making money a barrier to participation.

Through free training, the academy aims to help young people become:

Stronger. More disciplined.
More confident. More responsible.

Our Vision

Royal Sports Academy wants to create opportunities for more children to experience the benefits of martial arts.

The long-term vision is to use sports and martial arts as a platform for education, fitness, self-confidence, discipline and positive community development, while continuing to make training accessible to children who need the opportunity most.
