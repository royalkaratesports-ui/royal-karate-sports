# Kids Batches redesign

## Source and scope
The owner supplied five training features and five benefits, plus three photographs. All are included on kids-batches.html. Basic-to-advanced wording is grammar-edited without changing meaning. Belt gradation exams are stated every 5 months; passing or promotion is not guaranteed. National and international competition training is described as preparation, not guaranteed selection. Health, confidence and focus are training aims, not individual guarantees.

## Design
Primary surface: Decide/Learn for parents. Retain Royal's ink, warm-paper and vermilion palette, Barlow Condensed headings and Manrope body text. Use a photo-led split hero, compact programme overview, ruled training list, dark belt-exam band, editorial benefits/photo pair, community photo and parent FAQ/enquiry section. Mobile uses a readable single-column flow with the existing navigation and sticky trial shortcut. No new decorative motion.

Ten-tell design audit: 0/10. No tile grid, tech gradient, glass blur, artificial stats or all-centred composition. The five-month interval is actual owner-supplied programme information.

## Content boundaries
Free regular training is retained from supplied academy policy. Ages, times, session duration and equipment/exam/event requirements remain unconfirmed; no fees or schedules invented. Enrollment still prepares a local request draft and preselects Kids karate. No backend or deployment changes.
